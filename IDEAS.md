## BCP / "How things got made before digital"

Business continuity planning (BCP) angle: how did businesses run, and how did
everyday essentials get produced, before computers, CNC, and digital supply chains?

Possible directions:
- **Essential goods without digital tooling** — how important items like
  protective masks were made with manual/analog processes (patterns, jigs,
  hand tools, mechanical presses).
- **Basic consumer staples** — e.g. how toothpaste was formulated, mixed,
  filled, and packaged pre-automation.
- **Printable tie-ins** — 3D-printable analog aids: jigs, templates, molds,
  hand-crank mechanisms, cutting guides, or educational models showing the
  old process step by step.
- **BCP lens** — what a business would need to keep producing if digital
  systems went down (paper ledgers, manual tooling, mechanical backups).
